# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Finance operations staff creating customer orders, tracking settlement progress, and resolving overdue balances from desktop or mobile workstations.

## Product Purpose

Provide a fast, trustworthy workflow for creating orders, recording partial or full payments, and understanding exactly what remains due. Success means every amount is clear, every action preserves ownership and financial invariants, and payment errors explain how to recover.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, accountable.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative fintech dashboards, marketing-page composition, nested cards, ambiguous status colors, hidden calculations, novelty controls, and animation that delays operational work.

## Design Principles

1. Put money, status, and due dates at the center of every decision.
2. Make the fastest safe path the obvious path.
3. Explain validation failures with the exact corrective action.
4. Preserve ownership and financial invariants at every layer.
5. Keep dense operational information readable on any viewport.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA with semantic controls, visible focus, explicit labels, sufficient contrast, keyboard access, non-color-only statuses, and reduced-motion support.
